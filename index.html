Zoho Corporation

March 2021 – present · Chennai
Member Technical Staff · Backend engineer, CPaaS and distributed messaging platform
  • Architected the backend of the Arattai Business Platform from the ground up: data model, microservice layout, message workflows and the credit management system. It now runs at millions of messages in production.
  • Designed and delivered a unified omnichannel CPaaS framework that brings eight channels (WhatsApp, Arattai Business, Telegram, LINE, WeChat, Viber, Facebook Messenger and Instagram) behind one RESTful API, so onboarding a new channel follows a standard path instead of a bespoke integration.
  • Engineered WebRTC infrastructure for WhatsApp Business Calling, owning system design, data model, APIs, scaling, testing and rollout, and folded real-time calling into the messaging platform.
  • Built and own an external bot microservice (Java, Kafka, Redis) that routes conversations to bot frameworks (Zoho Zia Agent, Zoho Zia Bot, Zoho GC Bot and Google Dialogflow) at low p99 latency for several internal Zoho products.
  • Built event-driven, multi-threaded Kafka pipelines with consumer-group scaling, partition-aware routing and back-pressure handling for fault-tolerant, at-least-once processing of 2 million messages a day across tenants and 10+ data centres.
  • Scaled the broadcast pipeline from 25 to 550 messages per second at the application level and to 500 messages per second per phone number.
  • Wrote an end-to-end acceptance suite (JUnit, WireMock) that mocks carrier and partner APIs, cutting production regressions. Lead design reviews, run services on Kubernetes with Prometheus metrics and Grafana dashboards, and mentor junior engineers.

About

I'm Senthil Pitchappan V, a backend engineer in Chennai. I joined Zoho in 2021 straight out of an Electronics and Communication degree and have worked on one product area since: the infrastructure behind business messaging, now eight channels, 2 million messages a day and 50,000+ customers across 10+ data centres. I'm one of the main developers on that platform, owning several of its features and modules end to end rather than the product as a whole.

I like problems where correctness and throughput pull against each other, which is most of messaging. Outside work I rebuild pieces of that world in the open (Nudgeway is the clearest example) and spend time with local models, retrieval and agent tooling to understand them from first principles rather than through a framework.

Before Zoho I built IoT and Flask projects through college, reached the Smart India Hackathon national finals in 2019, and published a paper on an IoT surveillance robot.
